Keeping it safe

  • Prepsto keeps only a fingerprint of each key, never the key itself, so it's shown once.
  • A key does what you could do yourself, on the links you chose, and nothing else. Leaving a team cuts it off from that link straight away.
  • It can't delete anything, and it never sees senders' email addresses or their private tracking links.
  • What Claude does appears under your name. Replies to senders are off unless you allow them for that key.
  • Keys expire when you chose, are limited to , and every request is logged above.
