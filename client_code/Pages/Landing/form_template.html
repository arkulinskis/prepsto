// how it works

From "this is broken" to a story your team can build

Prepsto sits between your users and your backlog.

01

Drop in the panel

Share your link, or paste one script tag. A "Feedback" button appears and slides the panel in. Pick the colour, the question and the kinds of feedback you want.

02

Triage in seconds

Everything lands in one inbox with the page, device and rating. Sentiment, urgency and themes are suggested for you. Drag cards across a board, tag, star, comment.

03

Ship user stories

Select related feedback and draft a story: As a…, I want…, so that…, with acceptance criteria, team comments and client quotes. Export to Markdown, Gherkin, Jira or CSV.

// closing the loop

Support that never goes silent

The number one complaint people have about software is asking for something and never hearing back. With Prepsto, everyone who sends feedback can follow it.

  • A private tracking link: shown right after they send, and kept in the panel under "Your feedback".
  • Status and priority, live: Received, under review, planned, in progress, done: it moves as you triage, with no extra work.
  • Linked to the plan: when their idea becomes a story, they see it, and how many others asked for the same thing.
  • Public replies, two ways: answer them from the inbox; they can add details or answer your questions. Team comments stay private.

// faq

Questions

Is it free?

Yes, Prepsto is free while it is in beta. If we ever add paid plans, current links keep working and you will hear about it first.

What does the code add to my site?

A small script that draws the button and, when clicked, opens the panel in a frame served by prepsto.com. It sets no cookies on your site and loads nothing until someone opens it.

Who can read the feedback?

Only you, the owner of the link. The people sending feedback can't see each other's messages. Our Privacy & Terms page has the details.

Does it use AI?

Today Prepsto uses a built-in rules engine for sentiment, themes and story drafts, so nothing leaves the app. It is built so an AI model can take over those jobs later, and you can already export an AI-ready JSON bundle.

Can people see what happened to their feedback?

Yes. After sending, they get a private tracking link showing the status, the priority you gave it, the story it became and your public replies, and they can answer. You can turn this off per link.

Can Claude work on my feedback?

Yes. Make an API key on the Claude & API page (you choose what it may do and which links it sees), then Claude Code or any script can list your tickets, read them, update their status, leave team notes and, if you allow it, reply to the sender. Keys can be revoked at any time.

Can my users send feedback from their phone?

Yes. The panel is designed for phones first and works in any modern browser.

Can I export my stories to Jira or Linear?

Copy Jira markup straight into an issue, import the CSV, or paste the Markdown into Linear, GitHub or Notion.
